Understand the examination before starting your preparation.

What is UPPSC?

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC), Prayagraj, conducts recruitment examinations for various posts and services under the Government of Uttar Pradesh.

The Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services Examination, commonly known as UPPCS, is one of the major examinations conducted by UPPSC.

The official UPPSC website publishes examination notices, question papers, results, marksheets and other candidate-related information. Candidates should always check the latest official notification for the applicable recruitment rules.

UPPSC / UPPCS Examination Stages

The recruitment process is conducted through stages prescribed in the applicable notification.

Stage 1
Prelims

Preliminary Examination

Objective-type screening stage.

Stage 2
Mains

Main Written Examination

Descriptive written examination.

Stage 3
Interview

Personality Test / Interview

For candidates qualifying as per the applicable rules.
